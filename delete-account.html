Delete your Krysolara account

Krysolara: Space Invasion · Account and associated data deletion

In the game

Open Player Account → Delete My Account. Sign in if necessary and confirm the request. The app attempts to remove the Firebase account, private Firestore progress and public leaderboard record. You may need to sign in again before Firebase will permit account deletion.

Without the game

You can request deletion directly by emailing Atsi85@gmail.com with the subject Krysolara account deletion request. State the email address you used for the Krysolara account, but do not send your password. We'll verify that you own the account before taking action.

Once verified, we will process the request for deletion of the Firebase sign-in and online player records. Certain limited security or legal records held by providers may remain as required by law or provider retention policies. A device's local game save is not remotely deleted; use your Android app storage settings to clear it.

If you cannot access your account email, contact us to discuss available identity checks and the deletion request. Privacy policy: Read here.
