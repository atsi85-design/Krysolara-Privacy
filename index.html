Krysolara: Space Invasion
Privacy Policy

Updated 6 November 2026 · Support: Atsi85@gmail.com

We explain how Krysolara uses local game saves, optional accounts and online rankings, and Google advertising. Krysolara is a free-to-play arcade game; players do not have to register to play the core game.

1. Local game information

Your phone stores game progress, scores, earned crystals and fuel cells, unlocked ships, health, sound preferences and settings. This local data normally stays on your device. Removing the app or clearing storage can erase it. Online cloud sync currently covers total score, unlocked checkpoint and highest completed level; it does not back up purchased or earned resources or ship inventory.

2. Optional player registration and cloud saving

If you create a player account, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, a unique account identifier and authentication/security information. Firebase Cloud Firestore holds account-linked records for total score, checkpoint, highest level and timestamps. Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and are not kept in the leaderboard. You can play without an account, but you will not have online score sync.

3. Worldwide leaderboard

When you opt into public rankings by choosing a gamer name and verifying your email address, other players can see your gamer name, submitted score and highest level. The game does not publish your email address to the leaderboard. Scores are submitted by player devices, and they are not independently verified against gameplay. Please do not put personal information in your gamer name.

4. Advertising and consent

Krysolara includes Google's AdMob SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). When advertising is enabled and permitted, Google and its advertising partners may process device and network identifiers, approximate location inferred from network data, app interactions, diagnostics, consent signals and other data described by Google's applicable policies to serve, protect and measure advertising. A full-screen interstitial may appear after every second finished game attempt, including retries, on a results screen only. Display depends on consent, network, ad availability and app approval. Where available, the game shows an Ad Privacy Choices option. This build excludes the Android advertising ID permission, but other SDK/device data can still be processed.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy information.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

When available, the optional resource pack provides 5,000 Crystals and 500 Fuel Cells via Google Play Billing. It is a repeatable consumable one-time purchase, priced at A$0.99 for Australian players (other regions see their local Play price before confirming). Google handles payment and billing records; the game stores a hashed local purchase receipt reference and grants resources to the device wallet. We do not collect your payment-card details. Purchased and earned resources are not backed up to Firebase and may be lost if you clear data, uninstall, or switch devices. Google Play manages purchases and applicable refunds. No subscriptions are offered.

6. Data sharing, security and retention

We use Google Firebase for sign-in and optional cloud records, and Google AdMob for advertising. These services may process information outside Australia under their own privacy and security policies. Cloud records are retained while an account remains active, unless deletion is requested or required by law. Technical, fraud-prevention, security and backup information may be retained by service providers according to their policies and legal obligations. Firestore's security rules restrict private progress to the signed-in account; a selected public leaderboard entry is visible to other players.

7. Account deletion and privacy requests

Players can open Player Account → Delete My Account inside Krysolara when signed in. The app requests deletion of the player cloud record, public leaderboard entry and Firebase Authentication account. Firebase may require recent sign-in before account deletion succeeds; if a step fails, retry after signing in again or contact support. You can also request account and associated data deletion without installing the game: Delete your Krysolara account.

Account deletion does not clear files or saves already held on your phone; these can be removed with Android's app storage controls. Do not send us your password or payment-card information.

8. Age and choices

Krysolara is currently configured in Google Play for an intended audience aged 13 and older. Our advertising setup should be reviewed against age-related and regional requirements before production advertising is activated. Parents and guardians can contact support regarding questions about younger users.

9. Contact and changes

For access, deletion, privacy concerns or support, email Atsi85@gmail.com. We may update this policy as features change; the date above indicates its revision.
