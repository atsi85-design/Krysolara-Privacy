1. Game progress and settings

Krysolara saves information on your device so you can continue playing. This can include your level and score, earned diamonds/crystals and fuel cells, unlocked and equipped ships, upgrades, gameplay preferences, music and sound settings, and a gamer name if you choose to enter one.

In the current release-preparation version, this information is primarily stored locally on your device. It may be lost if you uninstall the game or clear its app data. Local game progress is not automatically a public leaderboard entry.

2. Internet connection

The planned Google Play release requires an active internet connection. The app may check connectivity to allow play and to request advertisements or other enabled online services. An internet connection does not guarantee that an advertisement will be available each time.

3. Advertising and information processed

When advertising is enabled, Krysolara uses Google AdMob and Google's advertising partners to deliver, limit, measure and protect ads. Advertising is intended to be shown only at the end of eligible game attempts, not during active gameplay. The current planned schedule is after every second finished game attempt, including retries, subject to ad availability and applicable consent choices.

Google's advertising technology may automatically collect or process:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, which can indicate approximate location;
  • device and advertising identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • app interactions, such as launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic, performance and fraud-prevention information; and
  • advertising consent selections and related privacy signals.

Google and its partners may use identifiers and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance, subject to applicable law and the choices available to you. We do not request your precise GPS location for game advertising.

For additional details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising consent and your choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, Krysolara will request applicable advertising consent or present privacy choices using Google's consent-management tools. You can accept, refuse or manage choices as offered by the consent message. If you decline personalised ads, eligible non-personalised or limited ads may still be shown.

Where available, you can revisit advertising privacy choices from the app's privacy settings. You can also manage or reset your device's advertising identifier through Android's privacy settings. The options offered depend on your location, device and configured version of the app.

5. Online accounts, worldwide rankings and optional purchases

The game is being prepared with optional online-account and leaderboard features, but public rankings and online account syncing are not currently operational in the test release. Entering a gamer name in an unavailable or preview leaderboard does not publish your score worldwide.

Similarly, a proposed optional resource pack is not currently available for purchase in the test release. All ships can be earned through gameplay. Before we enable player accounts, a public leaderboard or real-money purchases, we will update this policy to explain the additional information processed, relevant service providers, and account or purchase controls. Where account creation is offered, we will also provide the required account-deletion process.

6. Service providers and international processing

When enabled, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process the information described above. Android and Google Play may independently process device, installation, security or store information under their own policies. Information handled by these providers may be processed in countries outside your own, subject to their applicable safeguards and legal obligations.

We do not publish private device identifiers, support correspondence or locally stored game progress as a public leaderboard. Any future online profile or ranking will require separate disclosure before activation.

7. Data retention, access and deletion

Locally stored game progress and settings generally remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can contact us to ask about information you have sent directly to us, such as a support request, and request correction or deletion where applicable.

Google and its advertising partners retain information according to their own applicable policies and legal obligations. For information collected through Google services, consult the Google privacy links above and any controls presented by the service.

If we introduce player accounts in a future version, we will explain what account data is retained, how it can be deleted, and how to request deletion through the app and an external webpage before enabling registration.

8. Children and younger players

Krysolara is an arcade game. We do not ask players to provide a real name, home address or personal contact details merely to play. Any advertising controls for children or younger audiences must follow the game's declared target audience and applicable Google Play, AdMob and privacy requirements. Parents and guardians can contact us if they have a privacy concern involving a younger player.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's features, providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Significant changes will be communicated through appropriate in-app or other notices when required.
