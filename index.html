Game progress and settings

Krysolara saves your level, scores, earned Crystals, Fuel Cells, unlocked ships and selected game settings (such as music and effects) locally on your Android device. This allows your progress to be available when you open the game again.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may permanently erase this locally saved progress. The current Android app is configured not to include its data in Android's standard app backup. Keep this in mind before changing devices or reinstalling.

The Google Play release requires an internet connection for its online and advertising features. Local game information may be present on your device even when no player account is used.

Advertising and privacy choices

Krysolara integrates Google AdMob for advertisements. During development and internal testing, the game uses Google's test advertisements. Real advertisements are not enabled in those test builds. A separate production configuration may enable real ads later.

When advertising is enabled and a request is permitted, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and app information, interaction and diagnostic data, network information (which may indicate an approximate location), advertising identifiers where available and permitted, and consent choices. This information can be used to deliver, secure and measure advertisements in accordance with applicable rules and privacy choices.

An interstitial advertisement may be offered after every second finished game attempt (2, 4, 6, 8, and so on), including retries. Ads are shown only at the results screen, never in the middle of a game. An advertisement is not guaranteed to appear every time: consent, network status and ad availability affect whether one can be shown.

Where required, the game asks for advertising consent through Google's consent management system. If available for your region and circumstances, select Ad Privacy Options in the game's main menu to review or change your advertising choices. This option may not appear when it is not required.

More information is available in the Google Privacy Policy.

Accounts and leaderboards

Player account registration, cloud-synced progress and verified worldwide leaderboards are not active in the current test release. You do not need to create an account to play the core game.

If these optional services are introduced in a future release, Google Firebase may be used to process details such as an account email address, user identifier, authentication information, chosen gamer name, synced game progress and security information. If public rankings become available, the name and qualifying scores you choose to submit may be visible to others.

Before account registration is enabled, we will update this policy and provide appropriate access and account-deletion instructions.

Ships and proposed purchases

All 50 ships can be earned through gameplay using Crystals and Fuel Cells. Buying anything is not required to unlock the ships.

A proposed optional A$0.99 resource pack would contain 5,000 Crystals and 500 Fuel Cells. This pack is COMING SOON and is not currently for sale. It is a preview only; no real payment is taken for it in the current version.

If in-app purchases are introduced, they will use Google Play's billing system and we will update the purchase disclosures and privacy information before sales begin.

Third-party services

Depending on the release, features enabled and your choices, the game may use services from Google, including AdMob and its consent management system, Google Play services and potentially Firebase for optional future accounts and cloud features.

These providers may process information on systems outside Australia in accordance with their own privacy terms. Their storage and retention periods are not necessarily the same as the game's local save data. See Google's privacy information for more detail.

Data retention and requests

Locally saved progress and preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We do not currently offer live player accounts from which you can request a cloud account deletion.

For questions about personal information, access or deletion requests, or advertising privacy choices, email Atsi85@gmail.com. We can explain the options available for the relevant information and service. Third-party providers may separately offer their own privacy and deletion controls.

Please do not email passwords or payment card details.

Younger players

Krysolara is designed to be family-friendly. The Google Play release is configured with advertising restrictions intended for younger audiences, including age-related consent handling and limits on ad content. Actual ad availability still depends on the advertising provider, applicable rules and the release configuration.

We do not require a child or any other player to register an account to use the core game. Parents and guardians with privacy questions may contact us at the address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game changes, including if real advertising, online accounts, public rankings or purchases become available. We will publish revisions on this page and change the effective date when appropriate.

Privacy contact: Atsi85@gmail.com.
